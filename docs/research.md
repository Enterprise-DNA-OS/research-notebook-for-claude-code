LabArchives: US$28,750/year is 50 corporate Professional seats multiplied by the published US$575/user/year. It is a list-price scenario, not a customer invoice. No verified Enterprise DNA customer adoption or invoice was found in the available customer and CRM snapshots. No live customer system or credentials were accessed. Buyers score is therefore conservative. The vendor documents an Australia/New Zealand service region. Search results contain alternatives and pricing comparisons; volume was not measured and no volume claim is made.

Labfolder: its public pricing displays EUR52/user/month with annual upfront payment for industry, or EUR31,200/year at 50 users. A documented XHTML archive exists, but local buyer evidence was weaker. Its recent acquisition notice also needs consideration in a future comparison. Salesforce Order Management publishes contact-for-pricing; a qualifying annual invoice was not verified and its distributed orchestration scope is larger.
